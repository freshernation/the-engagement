# Week 1: Concept fence

## Allowed

- Everything you know about building AI systems from the core route, as background
- The client's `BRIEF.md`, and anything the customer tells you
- The five documents of this week, in order

## Not this week

- **Code.** Not a prototype, not "just a quick test". A prototype on Monday turns the
  interview into a demo, and a demo gets compliments, not facts.
- **The client's `CUSTOMER.md`.** See the README, rule 2.
- **Tool and vendor names in customer-facing documents.** The customer is buying an
  outcome. Model names, frameworks and databases belong in `DESIGN.md`, not
  `PROBLEM.md` or `PROPOSAL.md`.

---

## Why no code

The most expensive mistake in this job is building the right thing for the wrong
problem, quickly and well. This week exists to make that mistake on paper, where it
costs a day.
