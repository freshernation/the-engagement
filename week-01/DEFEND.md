# Week 1: Project 1 defence

Forty minutes. **The instructor plays the decision maker** from the client's
`CUSTOMER.md`. The student has sent `PROPOSAL.md` the evening before.

---

## Phase 0: The cold read (5 min)

Read only the summary, aloud. Then say what you understood you are being offered, what
it costs, and what you must decide. If any of the three is wrong, that is the first
finding, and it is the student's, not yours.

## Phase 1: Trace (10 min)

Pick five claims across the proposal: the cost today, the target, one non-goal, one
requirement, the estimate. For each: *"Where did this come from?"* The student opens
`NOTES.md` and points at the line. A claim with no line behind it is a guess presented
as a fact. Count them.

## Phase 2: The decision maker's questions (15 min)

Ask as the decision maker would, from the sheet:

- *"What does it cost per month after you leave?"*
- *"What happens the first time it is wrong, and who finds out?"*
- *"Why should I believe the number in your summary?"*
- Ask for one of the non-goals, firmly. Does the student hold the line, and explain it
  in the business's terms rather than the engineer's?

## Phase 3: The fact they missed (10 min)

Every client sheet has "only if asked" facts. Tell the student one they did not find.
Ask: *"What changes in your proposal?"* They must name the sections and the direction of
each change, without opening the file.

---

## Grading

| | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Cold read | You understood offer, cost and decision from the summary alone | You needed the rest |
| Trace | Four of five claims point at notes or a stated assumption | Two or more guesses |
| Questions | Held the non-goal, answered cost and failure in business terms | Agreed to the non-goal, or answered in jargon |
| The missed fact | Named the sections that change and how | "I would have to rewrite it" |
