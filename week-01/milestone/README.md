# Week 1 Milestone: Project 1: the proposal

> One page the decision maker can say yes to, built entirely on facts the customer gave
> you.

---

## The file

`PROPOSAL.md`. Under 800 words. It is not a summary of your four documents; it is the
one page that persuades someone who read none of them.

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Under 100 words. What they get, what it costs, what they must decide. With a number. If they read nothing else, this is enough |
| **The problem** | In their words, with what it costs today |
| **What we will build** | The chosen option, in business terms. No model or framework names |
| **What we will not build** | At least three non-goals, each with one line of why |
| **How we will know it worked** | Baseline, target, date, and how it will be measured |
| **Timeline and cost** | A range, in days and in money per month to run |
| **What we need from you** | At least three things: data, people, decisions, with dates |
| **Risks** | The two that matter, and what you will do |
