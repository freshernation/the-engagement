# The AI roles

One AI, five characters. Open a fresh chat for each session, paste the whole role file
as your first message, fill in the `[SQUARE BRACKETS]`, then talk normally.

| Role | File | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | `customer.md` | Week 1 Monday's interview, any follow-up, and week 2 Tuesday to Friday |
| **Stakeholder** | `stakeholder.md` | Friday of both weeks: the customer's boss, who pays and doubts |
| **Tutor** | `tutor.md` | You have been stuck for twenty minutes |
| **Editor** | `editor.md` | Every document, after its tests pass and before anyone else reads it |
| **Defend** | `defend.md` | Friday, on your milestone, before your instructor sees it |

## Two things that ruin this

> "Just write the proposal for me."

You get a fluent document built on facts you never collected, and you cannot defend a
sentence of it. The tutor and the editor both refuse. Do not go around them.

> Reading `CUSTOMER.md` "just to check".

The customer sheet is the answer key. The whole skill this course teaches is getting
those facts out of a person who does not volunteer them.

## The customer remembers only what you tell it

Keep one chat per client for the whole two weeks, so the customer remembers what it
has already told you. If you must start a new chat, paste `ai/customer.md`, the
`CUSTOMER.md`, and your `NOTES.md`, and say which day it is.

## What goes in your log

Every role ends with a `SIGNAL` block. Copy it into `logs/signal-log.md`.
