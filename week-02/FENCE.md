# Week 2: Concept fence

## Allowed

- **Everything from the core route**: your agent, your retrieval pipeline, your
  service, your tracing, your evals
- The five documents from week 1, which you may change, and must say when you do
- The client's `data/` folder

## Not this week

- **New technology.** No framework, database or service you have not used before.
  Learning a tool under a customer deadline is how engagements slip, and the customer
  pays for your learning curve.
- **Building past the Musts.** A Should built before every Must is accepted is time
  stolen from the date you promised.
- **The client's `CUSTOMER.md`.** Still.

---

## Why

Two weeks is the shape of most first engagements: enough to prove the approach on real
data, and short enough that every day's choice shows.
