## What you need to know

### One colour, said first

**Green**: on track for the target and the date. **Amber**: at risk, and here is what we
are doing. **Red**: will miss without a decision from you. Choose honestly. An amber
reported on Tuesday is a conversation. A red discovered on Friday is the end of the
engagement.

### The number, every time

Where the measure is against the target, with the date of the run. The same number in
every update, so the customer learns to read it.

### Decisions owed

Most delays in an engagement are a decision waiting on the customer: access to data,
someone's time, a yes on a trade-off. Name each one, who must make it, and the date after
which it costs the timeline. Then they own it.
