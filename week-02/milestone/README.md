## The files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `CASE_STUDY.md` | Under 1,000 words. The document you keep |
| `DEMO.md` | The demo script: at least six steps, one of which shows a failure on purpose |

### `CASE_STUDY.md`

| Section | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Under 80 words: the customer, the problem, the result with a number |
| **The ask and the real problem** | What they asked for, what you found, and how you found it |
| **What we built** | In business terms first, then one paragraph of how |
| **What we refused to build** | And why the customer agreed |
| **The result** | Baseline, result, number of cases, date. Traceable to a run |
| **What changed along the way** | The scope change and how it ended |
| **What I would do differently** | Honest. At least two things |
| **Evidence** | At least three links: the repository, the evals, the handover |

Use the client's invented name. When you do this for a real customer, change the name and
ask permission before you publish anything.
