# Week 2: Project 2 defence

Sixty minutes. **The instructor plays the customer for the first half and the decision
maker for the second**, from the client's `CUSTOMER.md`.

---

## Phase 0: The demo (15 min)

The student runs `DEMO.md`, live, on the customer's sample data. Do not interrupt.
Note the minute anything goes wrong and what they say about it. A failure they planned
to show, and explain, scores higher than a demo that never had one.

## Phase 1: The number (10 min)

From `EVALS.md` and `CASE_STUDY.md`: *"How many cases? Who agreed they were real? What
was the baseline? What is it now? Which cases still fail, and why?"* Pick one failing case
and make them explain it at the level of a single record.

## Phase 2: The change (10 min)

From `CHANGE.md`: argue for the option they did not recommend, as the customer would.
Do they hold, concede with a reason, or fold?

## Phase 3: The handover (10 min)

*"I am the engineer who takes this over tomorrow. It is broken. What do I look at
first?"* They answer from `HANDOVER.md` without improvising. Then ask for one thing on
the "not done" list and see whether they can say what it would take.

## Phase 4: The case study (10 min)

As the decision maker, read the first paragraph of `CASE_STUDY.md` only. *"Would you put
your name to every number in this?"* Then pick the result and trace it to a run.

---

## Grading

| | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Demo | Ran, and explained the failure it showed | Did not run, or hid a failure |
| Number | Traced the result to a run and a failing case to a record | "It's around 80%" |
| Change | Held the recommendation, or changed it for a stated reason | Agreed to everything |
| Handover | Answered from the document | Answered from memory, and the document disagreed |
| Case study | Every number traceable | One number nobody can trace |
