# Cut list

What this course deliberately does not teach, and where to get it.

| Cut | Why | Where to learn it |
|---|---|---|
| A real customer | No course can give you one. The AI customer is a rehearsal | Your first freelance client or your first FDE posting. See `CASE_STUDY.md` for how to win one |
| Contracts, legal terms, invoicing, tax | They vary by country and change. Get them from a professional, not a course | A chartered accountant, and a lawyer for the first contract you sign |
| Sales: pipelines, cold outreach, pricing strategy | A different job. An FDE is usually put in front of the customer by someone else | Not covered |
| Managing a team | Two weeks, one engineer | Not covered |
| New technology | Week 2 uses only what you already built. Learning a framework under deadline is how engagements fail | The core route |
| Large programmes: multi-month delivery plans, steering committees | The shape is the same as two weeks, repeated. Learn the two-week shape first | Your first real engagement |
