# Discharge summary: format

Every section is required. A section with nothing to report says "None".

1. **Case number, admission date, discharge date**
2. **Treating doctor**
3. **Diagnosis at discharge**
4. **Presenting complaint** (one or two sentences)
5. **Procedures performed** (with dates)
6. **Course in hospital** (no more than five sentences)
7. **Medications at discharge**: name, dose, frequency, duration, one per line
8. **Medications stopped during stay**
9. **Follow-up**: when, with whom, and what to watch for
10. **Signature**: treating doctor, with date and time. Left blank for the doctor.
