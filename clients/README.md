# The clients

Three invented companies. Any resemblance to a real one is a coincidence, but every
problem here is common, and every twist is one engineers in this job meet often.

| Client | Where | What they ask for |
|---|---|---|
| [Meridian Freight](meridian-freight/BRIEF.md) | Pune, logistics | "An AI chatbot for our drivers" |
| [Sahyadri Multispeciality Hospital](sahyadri-hospital/BRIEF.md) | Nashik, healthcare | "Use AI to summarise patient records" |
| [Kaveri Finance](kaveri-finance/BRIEF.md) | Mysuru, lending | "An AI agent that approves loans" |

Each folder holds:

| File | Read it? |
|---|---|
| `BRIEF.md` | Yes. It is the email that started the engagement |
| `data/` | Yes, from week 2 Monday. A sample the customer sent over |
| `CUSTOMER.md` | **No.** It is the AI customer's character sheet: paste it into `ai/customer.md` unread |

All names, numbers and records are invented. The data contains no real person.
