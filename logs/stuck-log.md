# Stuck log

Every time you get stuck, one entry. Thirty seconds each.

This is not busywork and it is not for marking. It is the only honest picture your
instructor gets of where you actually struggle. Your working code shows what went
right, and this shows what went wrong, which is the more useful half.

Copy the block, fill it in, newest at the top.

```
### [DATE]: [FILE]
- Doing:      what I was trying to make happen
- Expected:   what I thought would happen
- Got:        what actually happened (paste the error)
- Tried:      what I attempted, in order
- Resolved:   how it ended - my own / tutor / instructor / still stuck
- Minutes:    how long
```

---

### 2026-10-12: week-01/day-2/PROBLEM.md   *(example: delete this one)*

- Resolved:   my own: booked a 10-minute follow-up and asked about last Tuesday
